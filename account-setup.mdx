Your Berrytap account is the foundation for everything you do on the platform — whether you're running a paid community or learning inside someone else's. This guide walks you through creating your account, building out your profile, locking down your security settings, and understanding how to move between your creator and member roles.

<Note>
  You must be **13 or older** to create a Berrytap account. To sell content or receive payouts as a creator, you must be **18 or older**. By signing up, you confirm that you meet the age requirement that applies to how you plan to use the platform.
</Note>

---

## Create your account

Head to [berrytap.com/register](https://berrytap.com/register). You have two ways to sign up:

### Option 1 — Continue with Google

Click **Continue with Google**, select your Google account, and grant the requested permissions. Berrytap will pull in your name and email automatically. You'll land in your dashboard within seconds — no password to set or remember.

### Option 2 — Email and password

Fill in the registration form:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **First name** | Your given name |
| **Last name** | Your family name |
| **Email** | An address you check regularly — used for receipts, notifications, and account recovery |
| **Password** | At least 8 characters; mix letters, numbers, and symbols for strength |

Click **Create account**, agree to the [Terms](https://berrytap.com/terms) and [Privacy Policy](https://berrytap.com/privacy), and you're in.

<Tip>
  Already have an account? Go to [berrytap.com/login](https://berrytap.com/login) instead of re-registering.
</Tip>

## Build your profile

A complete profile builds trust with members and makes you easier to find. Head to **Settings → Profile** to fill in the following:

- **Display name** — the name members see in the feed, in live rooms, and on the leaderboard.
- **Avatar** — upload a clear photo or logo (square images work best). Your avatar appears on every post, comment, and course you publish.
- **Bio** — two to three sentences about who you are and what you're about. For creators, this is prime real estate: tell potential members exactly what they'll gain by joining your community.
- **Social links** *(optional)* — link out to your other platforms so members can find more of your work.

Hit **Save changes** when you're done.

## Notification preferences

Berrytap sends notifications by email and, where supported, via in-app alerts. Go to **Settings → Notifications** to control what you hear about and when.

<Accordion title="Creator notifications">
  - New member joined your community
  - New payment received or subscription renewed
  - Post or comment activity in your community feed
  - Live room starting reminders
  - Platform announcements and feature updates
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Member notifications">
  - New post or course published in a community you belong to
  - Reply to your comment or reaction on your post
  - Upcoming live room reminders
  - Renewal receipts and billing updates
</Accordion>

Turn off any category you don't need — you won't miss critical billing or security alerts, as those are always sent regardless of your preference settings.

## Account security

Keeping your account secure protects both you and your members. Here's what to do:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use a strong, unique password">
    If you signed up with email and password, make sure your Berrytap password isn't shared with any other service. Use a password manager if you can.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep your email address current">
    Your email is your account recovery lifeline. If it changes, update it immediately in **Settings → Account** so you never get locked out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review active sessions">
    In **Settings → Security**, you can see where your account is currently logged in and sign out of any session you don't recognise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch for phishing">
    Berrytap will never ask for your password over email or chat. If you receive a suspicious message claiming to be from Berrytap, forward it to [support@berrytap.com](mailto:support@berrytap.com) and do not click any links.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switch between creator and member views

Berrytap is a two-sided platform, and you can be both a creator and a member on the same account. Use the **role switcher** in the top navigation bar to move between:

- **Creator view** — your community dashboard, course builder, earnings, and member management.
- **Member view** — communities you've joined, courses you're enrolled in, and your personal feed.

Your progress, points, and enrollment history in communities you've joined as a member are fully separate from your creator analytics.

## Delete or deactivate your account

We'd hate to see you go, but your data is yours.

- **Deactivate** — temporarily hides your profile and communities without deleting your data. You can reactivate at any time by signing back in.
- **Delete** — permanently removes your account, communities, content, and member data. This action cannot be undone.

To request either option, email [support@berrytap.com](mailto:support@berrytap.com) with the subject line **Account deletion request** or **Account deactivation request**. The support team will process your request and confirm within a few business days.

<Warning>
  If you are a creator with active paying members, deleting your account will immediately end all active subscriptions. Make sure to notify your members before submitting a deletion request.
</Warning>
