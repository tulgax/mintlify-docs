Once you've joined a community or two, your account settings become your command centre. From there you can see everything you're subscribed to, manage how you pay, and cancel whenever you like — no hidden hoops, no surprise charges. Here's exactly how it all works.

## Viewing your active memberships

Sign in and go to your **Account Settings**. Under the **Memberships** section you'll see every community you've joined, the billing amount and frequency, the next renewal date, and the current status of each membership.

## How billing works

Berrytap supports two payment methods, and they renew differently:

- **Card (Visa/Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay)** — your card is charged automatically at the start of each billing period. You don't need to do anything; access continues uninterrupted as long as the payment goes through.
- **QPay** — a new invoice is issued at the start of each billing period. You pay it manually by scanning the QR code in your bank app. Your access continues once the payment clears.

## Updating your payment method

To swap to a different card, go to **Account Settings → Memberships**, select the community you want to update, and choose **Update payment method**. Enter your new card details and save. (Payment method updates apply to card payments only — QPay invoices are always paid fresh each period.)

## Cancelling a membership

Ready to leave a community or just need a break? Here's how:

1. Go to **Account Settings → Memberships**.
2. Find the community you want to cancel.
3. Select **Cancel membership** and confirm.

Your access continues until the end of the current billing period — you won't be cut off immediately. After that, the membership won't renew and you'll lose access to the paid content.

## Reactivating a cancelled membership

Changed your mind? Head back to the community page at [berrytap.com/discover](https://berrytap.com/discover) (or use the direct link if you have it) and click **Join** again. You'll go through the normal checkout flow to restart your membership.

## Refund policy

Berrytap membership payments are **non-refundable**, except where required by law. Individual creators may have their own refund policies for their communities — check the community's description or reach out to the creator directly.

If you think you were charged in error (for example, a duplicate charge or an amount that doesn't match what was shown at checkout), email [support@berrytap.com](mailto:support@berrytap.com) and the team will look into it promptly.

<Warning>
  Because payments are non-refundable, **cancel before your next billing date** if you don't want to be charged for the upcoming period. Cancelling after a charge has already gone through won't trigger a refund for that period.
</Warning>

## What happens if a payment fails

Life happens. If a card charge fails or a QPay invoice isn't paid on time, a **grace period** applies — you'll keep access for a short window while you sort out the payment. If the payment still isn't completed by the end of the grace period, your access to the community may be suspended until you pay. You'll receive a notification so you're never caught off guard.

<Note>
  Your **course progress is always saved**, even if your membership lapses or you cancel and later reactivate. Pick up right where you left off.
</Note>
