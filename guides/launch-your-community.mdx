You've got the knowledge, the audience, and the idea — now it's time to build the home for it. This guide walks you through everything you need to go from zero to a live, paid community on Berrytap, with your first members inside and your first payment landing in your account.

<Note>
  Your community will be live at **berrytap.com/c/your-community-name** — pick a short, memorable name that reflects your brand. You can set this when you create your community.
</Note>

## Get your community live

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Head to [berrytap.com/register](https://berrytap.com/register) and sign up with Google or an email and password. The whole process takes about two minutes — no credit card required to get started.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your community">
    Once you're in your dashboard, click **Create community**. Give your community a name, write a description that tells potential members exactly what they get, choose a category, and upload a cover image. Your cover image is the first thing people see — make it count.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a plan (or grab the 90-day founding offer)">
    Choose the plan that fits where you are right now:

    | Plan | Monthly fee | Platform fee | Best for |
    |------|-------------|--------------|----------|
    | Starter | ₮15,000/mo | 10% | New creators, under ~20 paying members |
    | Growth | ₮40,000/mo | 5% | Growing communities up to ~₮4,500,000/mo in member revenue |
    | Scale | ₮500,000/mo | 2% | High-volume creators built to scale |

    <Tip>
      If you're just starting out, take advantage of the **90-day founding creator offer** — your monthly plan fee and platform fee are waived for the first 90 days. It's the best time to launch, grow your first members, and earn revenue before paying anything to Berrytap.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your membership price">
    Decide whether your community is free or paid. If it's paid, set your price in MNT and choose your access model:

    - **Monthly access** — members pay a recurring fee to stay in (great for ongoing communities)
    - **One-time access** — members pay once and get permanent access (great for courses and cohorts)

    You can always adjust your price later. Starting lower while you're building social proof is a valid strategy.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your first piece of content">
    Give new members something to land on the moment they join. You have two great options:

    - **Create a course** — build your first module with a few lessons to show members the value of your program right away.
    - **Write a welcome post** — pin it to the top of your feed so it's the first thing every member reads.

    <Tip>
      Write a pinned welcome post that clearly explains what members get, what the community is about, what content is coming, and what they should do first. Something like: "Welcome! Here's how to get the most out of this community…" — it sets the tone and reduces drop-off in the first 48 hours.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Share your community link and invite your first members">
    Your community link is **berrytap.com/c/your-community-name**. Share it everywhere — your social bio, stories, DMs, email list. Don't wait until everything is "perfect." Your first 10 members care about you and your expertise, not a polished design.

    For your very first members, personal outreach works best. Message people directly and invite them in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go live to welcome your members personally">
    Open a live room in your community and go live to welcome your founding members. A short 20-minute welcome session — introducing yourself, walking through what's in the community, and answering questions — builds trust fast and makes members feel like they made the right call joining.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's waiting for your members inside

Once your community is live, your members get access to everything Berrytap includes out of the box: a social **feed** for conversation and updates, your **courses** and lessons, **live rooms** for calls and sessions, a **member directory** to connect with each other, and **gamification** — points, levels, and leaderboards — that keeps engagement high without you having to push it.

Payments are handled natively in MNT via **QPay and card**, so there's no friction between someone deciding to join and actually becoming a member.

Ready to build? Start at [berrytap.com/register](https://berrytap.com/register) — or reach out at [support@berrytap.com](mailto:support@berrytap.com) if you have questions.
