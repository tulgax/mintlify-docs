Running a coaching program on Berrytap means your clients get everything in one place: a community where they connect and stay accountable, a course where they work through your program framework, and live rooms where you coach them in real time. No juggling five tools. No lost Zoom links. No separate membership platform. Just one home for your entire program — under your brand.

## How the Berrytap coaching setup works

Berrytap gives you three building blocks, and they work together:

- **Community** — the ongoing space where your clients post wins, ask questions, and support each other between calls. This is where culture and accountability live.
- **Course** — your program framework structured as modules and lessons. Clients work through it at their own pace, and you can update it any time without re-sending anything.
- **Live rooms** — your weekly group coaching calls, built right into the platform. No external video tool, no extra link to share.

Together, they make a coaching program that's genuinely better than a one-to-one model. Your clients get peer support, async material, and live access — and you deliver real transformation at scale.

## Build your program

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your community and set the program price">
    Go to [berrytap.com/register](https://berrytap.com/register) and create your community. Name it after your program (or your coaching brand), write a description that explains the transformation clients get, and set your membership price in MNT.

    For a coaching program, you have two pricing options:
    - **One-time access** — clients pay once for the full program (best for fixed-duration programs)
    - **Monthly access** — clients pay monthly for ongoing coaching (best for long-term or evergreen programs)

    <Tip>
      Consider combining both: charge a **one-time fee for course access** (the program materials) and a **monthly fee for community + live calls** (ongoing coaching). This lets clients pay for the program upfront and continue receiving coaching on a recurring basis. More on this below.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build your course as the program backbone">
    Inside your community, create a course that packages your methodology into a clear structure. Use **modules** to represent phases of the program and **lessons** for specific techniques, worksheets, frameworks, or exercises.

    A solid starting structure might look like:

    - **Module 1: Foundation** — your core framework, client onboarding, goal-setting
    - **Module 2: The work** — the main content, week by week
    - **Module 3: Integration** — putting it all together, reviewing progress
    - **Module 4: What's next** — continuation, accountability, next steps

    Add video walkthroughs, written notes, downloadable worksheets, and any other resources your clients need. This becomes the material clients return to again and again — long after the live calls.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set a weekly live room schedule for group calls">
    Create a live room and establish a consistent schedule — for example, every Tuesday at 7pm. Post the schedule as a pinned post in the feed so clients always know when the next call is.

    Your weekly live sessions are where the real coaching happens: you review progress, answer questions, spot patterns across your clients, and help them move through blocks in real time. The community feed keeps the momentum going between calls.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Post weekly in the feed to drive accountability">
    Don't wait for clients to show up on their own. Drive engagement with intentional posts:

    - **Monday**: a weekly intention prompt ("What's one thing you're committing to this week?")
    - **Wednesday**: a mid-week check-in ("How is the work going? What are you stuck on?")
    - **Friday**: a wins thread ("Share one win from this week, big or small.")

    Consistent posting from you signals that the community is active and that you're paying attention — which keeps clients showing up.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use the leaderboard to spotlight your most active clients">
    Berrytap's leaderboard tracks points earned by members for engaging in the community — posting, commenting, reacting. Use it to publicly celebrate clients who are showing up consistently. A quick "Shoutout to [Name] for being in the top 3 this week 🏆" in the feed costs you nothing and means a lot to the client receiving it.

    Active clients get better results, and recognising their effort reinforces the behaviour you want to see across the whole program.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keeping clients accountable between calls

The feed is your accountability engine. A few things that work well:

- **Daily check-ins** — a simple prompt every morning ("What's your one priority today?") builds a habit of showing up
- **Weekly review posts** — ask clients to share what they completed that week before the live call, so the call is more focused
- **Progress milestones** — when a client finishes a module, acknowledge it publicly. It motivates them and signals to others what's possible

The more the community talks to itself — clients responding to each other, not just to you — the stronger the program becomes. Your job is to spark the conversation, not carry it alone.

## One-time + monthly: the pricing combination that works

For most structured coaching programs, the best pricing approach is:

- **One-time access** for the course (clients pay for the program materials and keep access forever)
- **Monthly access** for the community and live calls (clients pay monthly to stay in the coaching container)

This means clients always have their materials, and you build recurring revenue from the ongoing coaching relationship. Clients who finish the course but want to stay connected — they can. And new clients can join at any time without disrupting the program structure.

Set this up in your Berrytap dashboard by creating access tiers that reflect both options.
