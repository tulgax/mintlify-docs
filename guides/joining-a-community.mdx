Whether you're looking to level up your skills, find your people, or dive into a topic you love, Berrytap makes it easy to go from curious browser to active community member in minutes. Head to [berrytap.com/discover](https://berrytap.com/discover), pick something that excites you, and you're on your way.

## Join your first community

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to berrytap.com/discover">
    Visit [berrytap.com/discover](https://berrytap.com/discover) to see every community on the platform. You'll find communities across six categories: **Business**, **Education**, **Fitness**, **Technology**, **Creative**, and **Lifestyle**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse by category or scroll through all communities">
    Use the category tabs at the top to filter by topic, or scroll through all communities at once. Each card shows the community name, a short description, the number of members, and the price (free or a monthly amount in ₮).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click a community to see what's inside">
    Open any community to read the full description, see what's included — courses, live rooms, the member feed — and check the membership price before you commit to anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Join">
    Hit the **Join** button. If you don't have a Berrytap account yet, you'll be prompted to create one right then and there. Sign up with Google or with your email and password at [berrytap.com/register](https://berrytap.com/register) — it only takes a moment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment for paid communities">
    If the community has a membership fee, choose how you'd like to pay:
    - **QPay** — scan the QR code with your Mongolian bank app to pay instantly in MNT (₮).
    - **Card** — enter your Visa or Mastercard details, or use Apple Pay or Google Pay for a one-tap checkout. Card payments auto-renew each billing period so you never lose access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in — start exploring!">
    That's it! You now have full access to the community feed and discussions, all included courses, the member directory, and any scheduled live rooms. Dive straight in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Joining a **free community** skips payment entirely. Just create your account and hit Join — no card details needed, no hoops to jump through.
</Tip>

<Note>
  You must be **at least 13 years old** to create a Berrytap account, and **at least 18** (or the age of majority where you live) to purchase a paid membership. Free communities are open to all eligible members.
</Note>
