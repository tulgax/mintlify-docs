Courses on Berrytap aren't lonely PDFs sitting in a forgotten inbox — they live inside a thriving community where your students can cheer each other on, ask questions in the feed, and actually *finish* what they started. You get a full course builder, built-in live rooms, and local QPay and card payments priced in tögrög (MNT), all without stitching together five different tools.

## Why community + course beats a standalone platform

The dirty secret of standalone course platforms is that most students never complete the course. They buy, they watch one lesson, and life gets in the way. Pair that same course with a community and the whole dynamic changes — students feel accountable, they see others progressing, and they come back.

On Berrytap, your course and your community are one thing. The social feed is right there. Your live rooms are right there. That's not a nice-to-have; it's the reason completion rates go up.

## What's included in every course

Every course you build on Berrytap comes with the full set of building blocks:

- **Modules** — group your lessons into logical chapters or sections
- **Lessons** — add video, text explanations, and downloadable resources to each lesson
- **Structured progression** — students work through lessons in the order you set, so your curriculum lands the way you designed it
- **Community feed** — discussions, wins, and questions happen right alongside the course content

## Membership includes all courses — no separate purchase

When someone joins your community, they get access to every course you've published inside it. There's no separate checkout for individual courses, no upsell wall, no confusion. One membership, everything included. This means your pricing conversation with a potential member is simple: join, and you get all of it.

## Live rooms complement your courses

Some things are better live — a Q&A after a dense module, a weekly group coaching call, a cohort kickoff session. Berrytap's built-in live rooms slot right into your community so you can run those sessions without sending members to a separate video tool. Schedule a live room to go along with a course module and watch engagement spike.

## How students experience your courses

From a member's perspective, it's refreshingly simple. Once they join your community and pay their membership in MNT via QPay or card, your courses are right there waiting for them — no separate login, no extra app download, no hunting for an email link. They open your community, tap into the Courses section, pick up where they left off, and keep going. The social feed they've been posting in all week is just a swipe away.
