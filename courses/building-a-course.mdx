Building a course on Berrytap is less about wrestling with a complicated tool and more about getting your knowledge out of your head and into a structure your students can actually follow. You'll set up modules, drop in lessons with video, text, or resources, put everything in the right order, and hit publish — at which point every member of your community gets instant access. Here's exactly how to do it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your community dashboard and click New Course">
    Head to your community dashboard and look for the **Courses** section in your creator menu. Click **New Course** to open a fresh course editor. Everything stays inside your community — you won't be redirected anywhere else.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your course title, description, and cover image">
    Give your course a clear, specific title that tells students exactly what they'll be able to do by the end. Write a description that sells the transformation, not just the topic list. Upload a cover image — something bold and on-brand works best.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add modules">
    Modules are the chapters or sections of your course. Click **Add Module** and give each one a short, descriptive title. Think of modules as the big milestones in your curriculum: *Getting Started*, *Core Skills*, *Advanced Techniques*, *Putting It All Together*. Clear module titles set expectations and make the course feel approachable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add lessons to each module">
    Inside each module, click **Add Lesson** to build out your content. Each lesson can hold:

    - **Video** — upload a recorded lesson directly to Berrytap
    - **Text** — write explanations, instructions, or summaries in the rich text editor
    - **Resources** — attach PDFs, worksheets, templates, or any downloadable files

    Mix and match formats within a module to keep things varied and reinforce key ideas in different ways.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the lesson order">
    Drag lessons into the sequence you want students to follow. Berrytap enforces a structured path — students work through lessons in the order you define, so your curriculum lands exactly as you designed it. No skipping ahead, no missing a foundational concept because someone jumped to the advanced stuff.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your course">
    When you're happy with the content, hit **Publish**. Your course goes live to every member of your community instantly — no manual enrollment, no email blast required. Anyone who joins your community after you publish also gets access automatically as part of their membership.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for structuring great course content

A well-structured course keeps students moving forward instead of getting stuck or overwhelmed. A few things that consistently work well:

- **Short modules, shorter lessons.** Break your content into small, completable chunks. Finishing a lesson feels good — design for that feeling.
- **Lead with the win.** Start each module by telling students what they'll be able to do when they finish it.
- **One idea per lesson.** Resist cramming everything into one long lesson. Splitting ideas across multiple short lessons also gives you more checkpoints to celebrate progress.
- **Use resources strategically.** If a lesson involves a process your students will repeat, a downloadable template or cheat sheet dramatically increases how much value they get from it.

## Updating a course after publishing

Your course is never locked in. You can edit lesson content, swap out videos, reorder lessons, add new modules, or attach new resources at any time. Edits go live immediately — no republishing step, no version management headache. Your members always see the most current version of your course the next time they open it.

<Tip>
  Keep your video lessons under 10 minutes for the best completion rates. Shorter videos feel less intimidating to start, and students are far more likely to finish a 7-minute lesson than a 45-minute one. If you have a lot to cover, break it across two or three focused lessons instead.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Courses are included in your community membership — there's no separate course pricing or individual course checkout. When someone joins your community, they get access to every course you've published. Keep that in mind when you're pricing your membership: you're selling access to everything, not just one course.
</Note>
