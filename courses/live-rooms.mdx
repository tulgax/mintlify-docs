Live rooms are Berrytap's built-in video call feature, and they're exactly what they sound like: real-time video rooms that live inside your community. No Zoom link to generate, no Google Meet calendar invite to chase down, no "wait, what's the password?" from your members. You start a room, share it in your community feed, and your members join from their browser. That's it.

## What you can use live rooms for

Live rooms are flexible enough to power almost any kind of synchronous interaction between you and your community:

- **Group coaching calls** — get your whole cohort together to work through challenges, celebrate wins, and stay accountable
- **Weekly Q&As** — hold a regular open session where members can bring their questions and get answers in real time
- **Live cohort sessions** — run your course live, lesson by lesson, for a group starting and finishing together
- **Office hours** — show up at a set time each week so members know exactly when they can reach you
- **Workshops and masterclasses** — teach something new live and let members interact as you go

## How to start a live room

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your community">
    Open your community dashboard. Live rooms are accessible directly from your community — you don't need to navigate to a separate tool or settings page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Start Live Room or schedule one ahead of time">
    To go live right now, click **Start Live Room** and your room opens immediately. To schedule a session in advance, choose **Schedule Live Room**, pick your date and time, and add a title so members know what the session is about. Scheduled rooms show up in your community so members can plan ahead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the link with your members in the community feed">
    Once your room is live (or scheduled), post about it in your community feed. Tell members what the session is about, what to bring, and how long it'll run. A post in the feed is the fastest way to get eyes on it — your members are already there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Members join from their browser">
    Members click the link in the feed and join the live room directly in their browser — no app download, no account on a third-party platform, no friction. They're in your community already; the live room is just one more thing waiting for them there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices for running great live sessions

Getting the most out of live rooms is really about how you show up around the session, not just during it:

- **Announce ahead of time in the feed.** Post a day or two before your session so members can block off the time. A reminder post the morning of doesn't hurt either.
- **Set a regular cadence.** Weekly or bi-weekly live rooms that happen on the same day and time build a rhythm your community starts to rely on. Consistency beats frequency.
- **Start with a quick agenda.** Even two minutes at the top — "Here's what we're covering today, here's what I need from you" — makes the session feel intentional and focused.
- **Record for replay.** If replay is available in your live room setup, turn it on. Members who can't attend live still get the value, and recordings become evergreen resources you can point people to later.
- **Be present after.** The conversation that happens in the feed right after a live session is often just as valuable as the session itself. Stick around to reply.

## How members join a live room

From a member's perspective, joining a live room is zero-effort. They see your post in the community feed, tap the link, and their browser opens the room. No downloads, no sign-in to a new service, no hunting for a link in a separate email. Everything happens inside the community they're already part of.

<Tip>
  Post a recap in the community feed after each live session — key takeaways, anything you promised to share, a link to the replay if you recorded it. Members who couldn't attend feel included, members who did attend get a handy reference, and the recap post keeps the conversation going long after you've signed off.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Live rooms are fully built into Berrytap — your members don't need to download any extra apps or sign up for any other services to join. Everything happens right inside your community, in the browser they already have open.
</Note>
