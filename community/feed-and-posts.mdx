The feed is where your community actually comes alive. Think of it as a social timeline that lives inside your community — members show up, share what's on their mind, celebrate wins, ask questions, and cheer each other on. For creators, it's the fastest channel you have to make announcements, keep the energy going, and remind members why they joined. For members, it's the place to connect, be seen, and rack up points while doing it.

## What you can post

Every post in a Berrytap community feed supports:

- **Text** — share an update, ask a question, drop a hot take, or write a welcome message
- **Images** — post a photo, a screenshot, a design, a progress pic — anything visual that sparks a reaction

Posts appear in reverse-chronological order so the freshest content is always at the top. Members can comment on any post and react with emoji to show their vibe without typing a word.

## Reactions

Emoji reactions let members respond fast and expressively. See a 🔥 post? Hit the fire. Something hit the heart? Drop the ❤️. Reactions show up live on every post, and they count toward a member's engagement points — so reacting isn't just fun, it's rewarding too.

    ## Making your first post

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open your community feed">
        Go to your community dashboard and click into the **Feed** tab.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Click the post composer">
        Tap the **"Share something with the community…"** box at the top of the feed to open the composer.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Write your post (and add images if you want)">
        Type your message. To add an image, tap the image icon in the composer and upload from your device.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Post it">
        Hit **Post**. Your content goes live instantly for all members who have access.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    ## Pinning announcements

    Got something important — a welcome message, a schedule update, a big launch? Pin it to the top of your feed so it's the first thing every member sees when they open your community. Pinned posts stay fixed at the top no matter how much new content flows in below.

    To pin a post, open the options menu (the **···** icon) on any post you created and select **Pin to top**.

    ## Moderating the feed

    You're in full control of what lives in your community. As a creator, you can:

    - **Delete any post** — including posts made by members — from the options menu on that post
    - **Delete comments** — hover over a comment and use the delete option to remove it
    - **Remove members** — if someone consistently posts content that doesn't belong, you can remove them from the community entirely (see [Members & Roles](/community/members-and-roles))

    <Tip>
      Use your feed for announcements and welcome posts — not just content drops. A warm welcome pinned to the top of your feed makes every new member feel like they joined something real. Try posting a pinned "Start here" message that tells new members where to go first, what to expect, and how to introduce themselves.
    </Tip>

    ## Best practices for keeping your feed active

    A quiet feed can make a community feel abandoned. Here's how to keep the energy going:

    - **Post consistently** — even a short update, question, or behind-the-scenes photo is enough to spark conversation
    - **Ask questions** — "What's your biggest challenge this week?" gets more replies than a solo announcement
    - **Celebrate members** — when someone shares a win, react and comment. It signals to everyone that this is a community that shows up
    - **Share early access** — give your community a sneak peek at upcoming courses, live rooms, or announcements before you share anywhere else
    - **Welcome new members publicly** — a quick "Welcome, [Name]! 👋" post in the feed makes newcomers feel seen and encourages others to chime in

  <Tab title="For members">
    ## Making a post

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the community feed">
        Navigate to the community you've joined and tap into the **Feed** tab.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap the post composer">
        Tap the **"Share something with the community…"** box at the top.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Write your message or add an image">
        Type what you want to share. Add an image by tapping the image icon. Keep it real — this community is your people.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Hit Post">
        Tap **Post** and you're live. Your post is now visible to all members in the community.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    ## Commenting on posts

    Tap any post to open it and see the full comment thread. Type your comment in the reply box at the bottom and hit send. Comments are a great way to start real conversations and — bonus — they earn you points toward your community level.

    ## Reacting to posts

    Tap the emoji reaction button below any post to react. You'll see the current reaction counts, and your reaction is added instantly. It's the quickest way to show love — and it earns you points too.

    <Note>
      Posting, commenting, and reacting all contribute to your points total. The more you engage, the faster you level up and climb the leaderboard. Check out [Gamification](/community/gamification) to learn more.
    </Note>
