Great communities don't just happen — they're built on habits, and habits need a little fuel. Berrytap's gamification system turns everyday participation into a game worth playing: members earn points for showing up, level up as they grow, and compete on a leaderboard that makes your community feel alive. The result? Members who come back not just for the content, but for the community itself.

## How points are earned

Every time a member takes an action inside your community, they earn points. The more they engage, the faster they accumulate. Here's what earns points:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Posting" icon="pen" href="/community/feed-and-posts">
    Creating a new post in the community feed earns points. Sharing something valuable — a question, an insight, a win — gets rewarded.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Commenting" icon="message" href="/community/feed-and-posts">
    Leaving a comment on someone else's post earns points. The more you contribute to conversations, the more you accumulate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reacting" icon="heart" href="/community/feed-and-posts">
    Dropping an emoji reaction — ❤️, 🔥, or others — on a post earns points. Even a quick reaction counts as engagement.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Completing Course Lessons" icon="graduation-cap" href="/courses/overview">
    Working through course content inside your community earns points too. Finishing lessons shows up in your points total, rewarding members who are here to learn.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Levels: growing as a member

Points add up and push members through levels. As you accumulate more points, your level increases — and that level is visible on your community profile. Levels are a badge of commitment: a Level 12 member has clearly been around, shown up consistently, and contributed meaningfully to the community.

Levels create natural recognition without any effort from the creator. Members who are newer can see what's possible; members who've put in the work get visible credit for it.

## The leaderboard

The leaderboard is a ranked list of the most engaged members in your community, ordered by total points. It updates in real time and shows who's been showing up the most — whether that's posting daily, completing courses, or lighting up the reactions on every post.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="For creators">
    ## Using leaderboards to build momentum

    The leaderboard is one of your most powerful engagement tools — and it costs you nothing to use. Here's how to put it to work:

    **Spotlight your top members.** Give a shoutout in the feed to the person at the top of the leaderboard each week. A single post that says "🏆 This week's top member is [Name] — they've been absolutely crushing it!" does three things: it rewards the winner, signals to everyone else that engagement gets noticed, and gives the whole community something to rally around.

    **Run point-based challenges.** Announce a challenge — "Most posts this week wins a free coaching session" or "First to 500 points gets early access to the new course." Tying real rewards to leaderboard positions makes the competition feel meaningful.

    **Use the leaderboard in your onboarding.** When new members join, point them to the leaderboard in your welcome post. It sets expectations immediately: this is a community where people show up, and showing up gets recognized.

    <Tip>
      Drop a leaderboard shoutout in your very first welcome post when you launch. Something like: "We're just getting started — who's going to be the first to hit the top of the leaderboard? 🏆 Start posting, reacting, and completing lessons to earn your spot." It sets a tone of fun competition from day one and gives early members an identity to form around — the founding leaderboard champions.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="For members">
    ## Checking your points and level

    Your current points total and level are visible on your community profile. To check where you stand:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the community">
        Navigate to the community where you want to check your stats.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Go to your profile">
        Tap your avatar or name to open your member profile within the community.
      </Step>
      <Step title="See your points and level">
        Your total points and current level are displayed on your profile. You'll also be able to see how your activity has been building up over time.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    ## Climbing the leaderboard

    The leaderboard is updated in real time — every post, comment, reaction, and completed lesson nudges your rank. The fastest ways to climb:

    - **Post regularly** — even a short update or question earns posting points
    - **Comment on other people's posts** — adding to conversations gives you comment points and makes the community better for everyone
    - **React to everything** — it takes one tap and still earns you points
    - **Work through the courses** — completing lessons earns points in the background while you're learning

    <Note>
      Points are community-specific. Your points and level in one Berrytap community don't carry over to another. Each community is its own game — so if you're in multiple communities, you're competing on multiple leaderboards at once.
    </Note>
  </Tab>

## Why it works

Gamification isn't gimmicky when it's tied to real value. On Berrytap, every action that earns points is also an action that makes the community better — a post that helps someone, a comment that sparks a conversation, a lesson completed that builds real knowledge. Points and levels don't replace the value of the community; they make that value visible and worth celebrating.
