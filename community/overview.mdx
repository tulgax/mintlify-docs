## Free vs. paid communities

You choose how people get in. Berrytap supports both models with no extra setup:

- **Free communities** — anyone can join instantly. Great for building an audience, running a support hub, or getting started before you monetize.
- **Paid communities** — you set a price in MNT (tögrög), and Berrytap handles the checkout, renewals, and access control automatically. Members pay with QPay or card, on a monthly or one-time basis.

<Note>
  You can offer both access tiers by starting free and switching to paid later — or by keeping a free community and selling courses and live rooms inside it separately.
</Note>

## For creators: create your community

Getting your community live takes less than five minutes. Here's how:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to berrytap.com/register">
    Head to [berrytap.com/register](https://berrytap.com/register) and create your creator account. If you already have one, just sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your community">
    Give your community a clear, compelling name. This is the first thing potential members will see on the discover page, so make it count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a category">
    Choose the category that best fits your community: **Business**, **Education**, **Fitness**, **Technology**, **Creative**, or **Lifestyle**. This helps the right people find you on [berrytap.com/discover](https://berrytap.com/discover).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your price">
    Decide whether your community is free or paid. If paid, enter a monthly price in MNT (₮). You can also set a one-time access price. Berrytap handles checkout, renewals, and invoices from here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize and launch">
    Add a cover image, write your community description, and hit publish. Share your community link with your audience — or let members find you organically on the discover page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Write a strong community description before you go live. Tell potential members exactly what they'll get, how often you post, and who the community is for. Clarity converts browsers into joiners.
</Tip>

## For members: find and join a community

Discovering your next favorite community is just as simple:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit berrytap.com/discover">
    Go to [berrytap.com/discover](https://berrytap.com/discover) to browse every community on Berrytap. Filter by category — Business, Education, Fitness, Technology, Creative, or Lifestyle — or scroll through everything at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the community page">
    Click any community to see a full description, what's included (feed, courses, live rooms, member directory), the member count, and the membership price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join">
    Hit **Join**. Free communities let you in instantly. Paid communities take you through a quick QPay or card checkout — all priced in MNT, no currency surprises.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start exploring">
    You're in. Jump into the feed, browse the courses, check the member directory, and introduce yourself. Your points counter starts the moment you engage.
  </Step>
</Steps>
