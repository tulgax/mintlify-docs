A community is only as strong as the people in it — and Berrytap gives you the tools to make sure the right people get in, feel welcomed, and have access to exactly what they've paid for. Whether you're running a free open community or a tightly gated paid membership, everything about who sees what is in your hands.

## How membership access works

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Free communities">
    Free communities are open to anyone with a Berrytap account. Members can join instantly — no payment, no approval required. Once in, they have access to the community feed, member directory, and any courses or live rooms you've made available to free members.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Paid communities">
    Paid communities require an active membership to access any content. When someone visits your community page, they'll see a preview of what's included — but the feed, courses, live rooms, and member directory are locked until they complete checkout. Berrytap enforces this automatically: access is granted the moment payment clears and revoked if a subscription lapses.

    Members pay in MNT (₮) using QPay or card. They can join on a **monthly subscription** (which auto-renews) or a **one-time access** plan, depending on how you've configured your community.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
  Make sure your membership price is set correctly **before** you share your community join link publicly. Once paying members are in, changing your price only affects new joiners — existing members keep their original rate. Double-check your pricing in your community settings before you launch.
</Warning>

## The member directory

Every Berrytap community includes a member directory — a browsable list of everyone who has joined. Members can see each other's profiles, which helps real connections form and makes the community feel like a real place rather than a content library.

As a creator, the directory is also how you keep track of who's in your community. You can see member counts at a glance from your dashboard.

## Inviting members

You don't have to wait for members to find you organically. There are two ways to bring people in:

- **Share your community link** — every community has a unique public URL (e.g. `berrytap.com/c/your-community`). Drop it in your social bio, email newsletters, or anywhere your audience hangs out. Anyone who clicks it lands on your community page and can join in seconds.
- **Discover page** — your community is listed on [berrytap.com/discover](https://berrytap.com/discover), where people browsing by category can find you. Make sure your name, category, and description are sharp to stand out.

<Tip>
  The most effective invite is a personal one. Send your community link directly to people you already know would benefit, with a one-line message about why they should join. Warm invites convert far better than cold links.
</Tip>

## Member roles and what they control

Berrytap lets you define what members can do inside your community. Roles determine who can post to the feed, who can see certain content, and how access is structured. As a creator, you hold the highest level of access — you can post, moderate, manage members, edit settings, and see everything.

Members can post to the feed and comment by default. If you need tighter control — for example, limiting who can start new posts vs. who can only comment — you can manage these permissions from your community settings.

## Gated content and access control

Gated content means only paying (or approved) members can see it. Berrytap enforces this automatically for paid communities — you don't need to manually lock anything. The moment someone's membership expires or is cancelled, their access is removed.

For free communities, all content is visible to any member. If you want to offer a mix — some content free, some premium — consider structuring that through separate courses or live rooms with their own pricing rather than within a single community tier.

## Removing or banning a member

Sometimes people don't belong. You can remove a member from your community at any time:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your member list">
    Open your community dashboard and navigate to the **Members** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the member">
    Search by name or scroll through the list to find the person you want to remove.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove them">
    Open their profile options and select **Remove from community**. Their access is revoked immediately and they will no longer be able to view the feed, courses, or any other community content.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Members can cancel their own paid membership at any time from their account settings — no need to contact you. When they cancel, their access continues until the end of the current billing period, then stops automatically. You don't need to do anything on your end. Monthly subscriptions auto-renew until a member cancels.
</Note>
