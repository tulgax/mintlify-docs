The Berrytap team is small, but they're genuinely here for you. Whether you've hit a technical snag, have a billing question, or just can't figure something out, there's a clear path to getting help. Here's how to reach them.

## Email support

For account issues, billing questions, or technical problems, email the team directly at [support@berrytap.com](mailto:support@berrytap.com).

To get the fastest, most useful response, include the following in your email:

- **Your account email address** — so the team can find your account quickly.
- **A clear description of the issue** — what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and when it occurred.
- **Any error messages or screenshots** — copy the exact error text if you can, or attach a screenshot. This saves a lot of back-and-forth.

## Community support

Join the official Berrytap support community at [berrytap.com/c/berrytap](https://berrytap.com/c/berrytap). It's a great place to:

- Get **product updates** straight from the Berrytap team.
- Find **tips and how-tos** from other creators and members.
- Ask questions and get help from the broader Berrytap community.

## Response times

The team aims to reply to all support emails within **1–2 business days**. They're a small crew, so there may be a short wait during busy periods — but every message gets a real, human response.

<Tip>
  For the fastest answers to common questions, check the [FAQ](/help/faq) and [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) pages first. Most issues have a step-by-step fix already written up, and you won't have to wait for a reply.
</Tip>
