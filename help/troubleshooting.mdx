---
title: "Fix common Berrytap login, payment, and access issues"
sidebarTitle: "Troubleshooting"
description: "Can't log in? Payment not going through? Find step-by-step fixes for the most common Berrytap issues — and know when to reach out to support."
---

Most hiccups on Berrytap have a quick fix. Work through the relevant section below and you'll likely be back on track in minutes. If something isn't covered here, email [support@berrytap.com](mailto:support@berrytap.com) and the team will help you out.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't log in">
    Try these steps in order:

    1. Double-check you're using the correct email address — it's easy to have multiple.
    2. Use the **Forgot password** flow at [berrytap.com/forgot-password](https://berrytap.com/forgot-password) to reset your password. Check your spam folder if the reset email doesn't arrive within a few minutes.
    3. If you originally signed up with **Continue with Google**, use that button to sign in — there's no separate password for Google-linked accounts.
    4. Still stuck? Email [support@berrytap.com](mailto:support@berrytap.com) with your account email address and a description of what you're seeing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My QPay payment didn't go through">
    QPay payments need a fresh QR scan each time. If yours didn't work:

    1. Make sure you're scanning the **correct, current QR code** — don't use a screenshot from a previous invoice.
    2. Check that your bank account has **sufficient balance** to cover the membership amount.
    3. Confirm the payment hasn't already gone through by checking your bank app's transaction history before trying again.
    4. If the issue persists, try **paying by card** (Visa/Mastercard, Apple Pay, or Google Pay) instead. You can switch payment methods in your account settings.
    5. Still no luck? Email [support@berrytap.com](mailto:support@berrytap.com) with your account email and the community name.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My card was declined">
    A declined card is usually one of a few things:

    1. **Verify your card details** — card number, expiry date, and CVV. A single wrong digit will cause a decline.
    2. **Check with your bank** — some banks block online or international transactions by default. A quick call or app notification approval often resolves this.
    3. **Try a different payment method** — Apple Pay and Google Pay can be more reliable for mobile payments and are worth trying if your card keeps declining.
    4. If you're still seeing a decline after checking the above, contact [support@berrytap.com](mailto:support@berrytap.com).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm not seeing the courses in my community">
    If you joined a paid community but courses aren't showing up:

    1. Go to **Account Settings → Memberships** and confirm your membership status shows as **Active**.
    2. If your payment is still pending or your QPay invoice hasn't been paid yet, complete the payment — course access is granted once payment clears.
    3. Try refreshing the page or signing out and back in.
    4. If your membership is active and courses still aren't visible, email [support@berrytap.com](mailto:support@berrytap.com) with your account email and the community name.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My members aren't receiving QPay invoices">
    QPay invoices are issued automatically at the start of each billing period — members receive them via the notification they have set up with their bank or through the Berrytap platform. If members are asking where their invoice is:

    1. Remind them to check their Berrytap notifications and their bank app.
    2. Post a reminder in the **community feed** prompting members to check for and pay their renewal invoice.
    3. If invoices genuinely aren't being generated, contact [support@berrytap.com](mailto:support@berrytap.com) with your community name and the billing date in question.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I haven't received my payout">
    Payouts on Berrytap are reviewed and processed manually by the team. Here's what to check:

    1. **Allow a few business days** after requesting a payout — manual review takes time.
    2. Go to your **Creator Settings** and verify that your bank account details (account number, bank name) are entered correctly. An error there is the most common cause of a missing payout.
    3. Check your bank account — sometimes transfers arrive with a slight delay depending on your bank.
    4. If it's been more than 5 business days and nothing has arrived, email [support@berrytap.com](mailto:support@berrytap.com) with your creator account email and the payout amount.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I accidentally posted something — how do I delete it?">
    No stress — deleting a post is quick:

    1. Find the post in the **community feed**.
    2. Click the **three-dot menu** (⋯) in the top-right corner of the post.
    3. Select **Delete** and confirm.

    The post will be removed immediately. If you're a creator and need to remove a member's post, the same three-dot menu is available to you as the community owner.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I reset my password?">
    Go to [berrytap.com/forgot-password](https://berrytap.com/forgot-password), enter the email address linked to your account, and hit **Send reset link**. You'll receive an email with a secure link to set a new password — check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

    If you signed up using **Continue with Google**, you don't have a Berrytap password — sign in with Google instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
