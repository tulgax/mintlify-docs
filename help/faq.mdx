Got questions? Great — here are the answers. These cover the most common things members and creators ask about Berrytap. If you don't find what you're looking for, check out the [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) page or [get in touch](/help/contact).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Berrytap?">
    Berrytap is an all-in-one platform where creators build paid communities — complete with courses, a social feed, live rooms, and membership payments — and where members join those communities to learn, connect, and grow. Everything lives in one place, priced in Mongolian tögrög (₮, MNT). You can explore what's available at [berrytap.com/discover](https://berrytap.com/discover).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I create a community?">
    Sign up for a Berrytap account at [berrytap.com/register](https://berrytap.com/register), then follow the prompts to set up your community — give it a name, description, cover image, and decide whether it's free or paid. You can add courses, set up live rooms, and invite your first members all from your creator dashboard. You must be 18 or older to sell access to a community.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do my members pay?">
    Members can pay using **QPay** (scan a QR code with a Mongolian bank app) or **card** (Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, or Google Pay). All payments are processed in MNT (₮). Card payments can be set to auto-renew; QPay issues a fresh invoice each billing period for members to pay manually.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much does Berrytap cost as a creator?">
    Berrytap charges a monthly platform fee plus a percentage of your member revenue:

    - **Starter** — ₮15,000/mo + 10% platform fee (you keep 90%)
    - **Growth** — ₮40,000/mo + 5% platform fee (you keep 95%)
    - **Scale** — ₮500,000/mo + 2% platform fee (you keep 98%)

    It's free to set up — you only share a fee once you start earning. Payment processing fees from providers may also apply.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When and how do I get paid?">
    Your earnings accumulate in your creator dashboard as members pay. You can request a payout to the bank account you've saved in your creator settings. Payouts are reviewed and transferred manually by the Berrytap team — allow a few business days. Make sure your bank account details are accurate to avoid delays.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need any technical skills to start?">
    Not at all. Berrytap is built for creators, not developers. Setting up a community, adding courses, and scheduling live rooms all happen through a simple, visual interface — no code required. If you can write a social media post, you can run a Berrytap community.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What can I sell on Berrytap?">
    You can sell access to a **paid community** (recurring monthly memberships), which can include structured **courses**, a social **feed**, **live rooms**, and a **member directory**. You can also offer your community for free if you prefer. One-time access purchases are also supported alongside recurring subscriptions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I offer a free community?">
    Absolutely. Setting your community price to free means anyone with a Berrytap account can join with no payment required. It's a great way to grow your audience before introducing paid tiers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can members access courses after cancelling?">
    Once a membership is cancelled and the current billing period ends, members lose access to the community's paid content, including courses. However, their course progress is saved — so if they reactivate later, they can pick up right where they left off.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods can my members use?">
    Members can pay with **QPay** (Mongolian bank QR scan), **Visa**, **Mastercard**, **Apple Pay**, or **Google Pay**. All transactions are denominated in MNT (₮).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Berrytap available outside Mongolia?">
    Berrytap is live at [berrytap.com](https://berrytap.com) and accessible globally. That said, the platform is built around Mongolian tögrög (₮, MNT) pricing and QPay — a local payment method — so it's best suited to communities based in or targeting Mongolia. Card payments (Visa/Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay) work internationally; your bank handles any currency conversion.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    Email the Berrytap team at [support@berrytap.com](mailto:support@berrytap.com). You can also join the Berrytap support community at [berrytap.com/c/berrytap](https://berrytap.com/c/berrytap) for product updates and community help. The team is small but responsive — expect a reply within 1–2 business days.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
