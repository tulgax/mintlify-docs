Think of it as your community hub, course platform, live-session studio, and payment system — all wired together so nothing falls through the cracks. Whether you're a coach running group programs, a course creator who wants learners to stick around, or a curious member ready to level up, Berrytap is built for you.

## How it works

### For creators

1. **Create your community** — sign up, name your space, and add a cover image.
2. **Build your content** — add courses, post to the feed, and schedule live rooms.
3. **Set your price** — charge a monthly subscription or offer free access, in MNT.
4. **Grow and earn** — invite members, watch the community grow, and receive payouts with low, transparent fees.

### For members

1. **Discover communities** — browse [berrytap.com/discover](https://berrytap.com/discover) to find spaces that match your interests.
2. **Join and pay locally** — sign up with Google or email, then pay with QPay or card in MNT.
3. **Learn and connect** — explore courses, join live sessions, post to the feed, and earn points as you engage.

## Why Berrytap?

Creators in Mongolia were stitching together a chat group, a course tool, and a payment link — losing members and money in the gaps. The existing platforms were priced in dollars and never supported the way people here actually pay. Berrytap fixes that: one connected system, local payments in tögrög, and fees that only apply when you actually earn. No upfront risk, no technical setup headaches.

<Note>
  Berrytap is built for Mongolia first — priced in MNT and supporting QPay — with the ambition to open its doors to creators and members everywhere.
</Note>
