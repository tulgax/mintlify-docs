Getting started on Berrytap is fast. Creators can go from a blank account to a live, paid community in one sitting — no technical skills required, no external tools to wire up. Members can discover and join a community in just a few clicks. Follow the path that fits you below.

---

## For creators: launch your community

<Tip>
  Founding creators get their first **90 days completely free** — no credit card required to start. You only share a percentage of revenue once your community is earning. It's the lowest-risk way to find out if your idea resonates.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Head to [berrytap.com/register](https://berrytap.com/register) and sign up in seconds.

    - **Continue with Google** — one click, no password to remember.
    - **Email and password** — enter your first name, last name, email address, and a secure password, then click **Create account**.

    You'll land straight in your new Berrytap dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your community and make it yours">
    Give your community a clear, memorable name — something your ideal member would search for. Then:

    - Write a short **description** (two to three sentences) that tells visitors exactly what they'll get by joining.
    - Upload a **cover image** that reflects your brand or topic.
    - Add a **community icon** or profile photo so members recognise you in the feed.

    A polished first impression converts curious visitors into paying members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your plan or start free">
    Pick the plan that matches where your community is today:

    | Plan | Monthly price | Platform fee | Best for |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | **Starter** | ₮15,000/mo | 10% | New creators, under ~20 paying members |
    | **Growth** | ₮40,000/mo | 5% | Growing communities up to ~₮4,500/mo revenue |
    | **Scale** | ₮500,000/mo | 2% | High-volume communities at scale |

    Not sure yet? Your first 90 days are free — you can upgrade any time from **Settings → Plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your membership price">
    Decide how members will access your community:

    - **Free** — open access, great for building an audience before you monetise.
    - **Paid subscription** — set a monthly price in MNT (e.g. ₮29,000/mo). Members pay via QPay or card.
    - **One-time access** — charge a single fee for a course or cohort.

    All prices are entered and displayed in tögrög (₮), so your members always know exactly what they're paying.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite members and share your link">
    Your community is live — now fill it up.

    - Copy your **community link** from the dashboard and share it anywhere: social media, messaging apps, email newsletters.
    - Use the built-in **invite by email** tool to bring in your first members directly.
    - Post something to the **community feed** straight away so new arrivals have something to react to.

    The first few members make it feel real. Start with people who already know and trust you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## For members: join a community

You don't need to be a creator to get value from Berrytap — the platform is packed with communities to learn from and people to connect with.

1. **Discover communities** — visit [berrytap.com/discover](https://berrytap.com/discover) and browse by topic or search for something specific.
2. **Sign in or create an account** — click **Join** on any community page. If you don't have an account yet, you'll be prompted to sign up with Google or email. It's free.
3. **Pay securely in MNT** — for paid communities, complete checkout with QPay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Visa, or Mastercard. Prices are always in tögrög — no currency conversion surprises.
4. **Start engaging** — introduce yourself in the feed, enroll in available courses, and join the next live room. Earn points as you participate and climb the community leaderboard.

<Note>
  Looking for a specific community? Ask your creator for their direct community link — it's the fastest way to land in the right place without browsing.
</Note>
