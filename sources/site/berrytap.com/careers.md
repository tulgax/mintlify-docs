# Build the platform creators rely on

We're a small team helping creators in Mongolia and beyond turn what they know into a living. If that's the kind of work you want to do, we'd love to hear from you.

## How we work

### Ship and learn

Small team, real users, short feedback loops. We put work in front of creators quickly and improve from what we see.

### Own the outcome

Everyone here carries a problem end to end — from the idea to the details creators actually feel.

### Close to creators

We talk to the people who use Berrytap every week. Their reality, not our assumptions, sets the roadmap.

### Build for the long run

We're building something creators can rely on for years, so we sweat quality, trust, and the boring-but-important details.
