## Berrytap vs Patreon at a glance

| Feature | Berrytap | Patreon |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local payments | QPay + card, priced in MNT | Card only |
| Platform fee | Keep up to 98% | 8–12% + card fees |
| Courses | Built in | Not built for it |
| Community feed | Yes | Posts only |
| Live rooms | Built-in live rooms | No |
| Gamification | Points, levels & leaderboards | No |
| Support | Local, in Mongolia | English only |

## Why creators choose Berrytap

### A community, not just a paywall

Members get a feed, courses, events, and live rooms — a reason to stay subscribed, not just a pledge.

### Keep more of every pledge

Low platform fees and local payments mean more of each membership reaches you.

### Local checkout

QPay and MNT so supporters in Mongolia can back you without a foreign card.
