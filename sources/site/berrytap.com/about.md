About us

# Helping creators turn what they know into a living

Berrytap brings your community, courses, and income together in one place — so you can focus on your members instead of juggling tools.

## Why we built Berrytap

Creators in Mongolia were stitching together a chat group here, a course tool there, and a payment link somewhere else — losing members and money in the gaps. The tools that promised to fix it were built for somewhere else, priced in dollars, and never spoke the way people here actually pay.

So we built **Berrytap**: one platform where a creator can run a paid community, sell courses, host live rooms, and get paid — with QPay and card checkout, priced in tögrög. No plugins to wire up, no separate checkout to maintain, no revenue slipping through the cracks.

We're just getting started, and the mission is simple — make it genuinely easy to build a community and earn from it, wherever your members are.

## What we care about

### Creators first

Every decision starts with the person building the community. If it doesn't help them earn a living or serve their members, we don't ship it.

### Fair by default

Low, transparent fees taken only from what creators actually collect — with local QPay and card checkout so no member is turned away.

### Local, then global

We build for creators in Mongolia first, priced in tögrög and fluent in how people here pay — then open the doors to members anywhere.

### One place, less busywork

Community, courses, live rooms, and payments in a single connected system, so creators spend their time teaching, not stitching tools together.
