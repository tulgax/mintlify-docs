## Get up and running as a creator

New to Berrytap? Follow these four steps to go from zero to a live, paid community.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [berrytap.com/register](https://berrytap.com/register) using Google or email — it takes under a minute. Your first 90 days are free as a founding creator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your community">
    Name your community, write a short description, and upload a cover image so members know exactly what they're joining.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your content">
    Build your first course, schedule a live room, or simply post to your community feed to give members a reason to show up on day one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your members">
    Share your community link, set a free or paid membership price in MNT, and watch your community come to life.
  </Step>
</Steps>
