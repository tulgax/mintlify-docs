Running a paid community on Berrytap costs two things: a flat monthly plan fee and a small percentage of the membership revenue you collect from your members. That's it — no hidden charges, no USD exchange rate surprises, and no per-seat fees for every new member who joins. Pick the plan that fits where you are today, and upgrade whenever your community is ready.

## The three creator plans

| Plan | Monthly fee | Platform fee | You keep | Best for |
|------|-------------|--------------|----------|----------|
| **Starter** | ₮15,000/mo | 10% | 90% | New creators, under ~20 paying members |
| **Growth** ⭐ | ₮40,000/mo | 5% | 95% | ~20 members, up to ~₮6M/mo in member revenue |
| **Scale** | ₮500,000/mo | 2% | 98% | High-revenue creators, ₮6M+/mo in member revenue |

> ⭐ **Growth** is Berrytap's most popular plan.

<Tip>
  Not sure where to start? Go with **Starter** if you're just launching or have fewer than 20 paying members. Move to **Growth** once your monthly member revenue is comfortably covering the plan fee difference — the lower platform fee pays for itself fast.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Every plan fee is charged in Mongolian tögrög (₮, MNT). You'll never wake up to a bigger bill because of USD/MNT fluctuations — what you see is what you pay, every month.
</Note>

## How to calculate what you'll keep

Your take-home from each billing cycle is straightforward:

1. **Add up your total member revenue** collected that period.
2. **Subtract the platform fee** (your plan's %).
3. **What's left is yours** — minus the flat monthly plan fee billed separately.

**Example — Growth plan with 10 paying members:**

| | Amount |
|---|---|
| 10 members × ₮30,000/mo | **₮300,000 collected** |
| Platform fee (5% of ₮300,000) | − ₮15,000 |
| **You receive** | **₮285,000** |
| Growth plan monthly fee (billed separately) | − ₮40,000 |
| **Net in your pocket** | **₮245,000** |

As your member count grows, the math swings strongly in your favor — especially on Growth and Scale where the platform fee is 5% or 2%. A creator on Scale with ₮10,000,000/mo in member revenue pays just ₮200,000 in platform fees (2%), keeping ₮9,800,000 before the flat plan fee.
