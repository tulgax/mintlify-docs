Your Berrytap plan grows with you. Whether your community just hit a new milestone and you're ready to unlock lower platform fees, or you need to step back temporarily, you're in full control of your plan from your creator dashboard. Here's everything you need to know about managing your plan, understanding auto-renewal, and what to expect if you ever need to cancel.

---

## View your current plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your creator dashboard">
    Log in at [berrytap.com](https://berrytap.com) and go to your creator dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Settings → Billing">
    In the left-hand navigation, click **Settings**, then select the **Billing** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your plan details">
    You'll see your current plan (Starter, Growth, or Scale), your next renewal date, and your billing history. Your current platform fee percentage is shown here too.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Upgrade your plan

Upgrading makes sense when your member revenue is growing and a lower platform fee would save you more than the higher monthly fee costs. For example, switching from Starter (10% fee) to Growth (5% fee) starts saving you money the moment your monthly member revenue exceeds ₮500,000.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings → Billing">
    From your creator dashboard, navigate to **Settings → Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Change plan'">
    Select **Change plan** next to your current subscription.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your new plan">
    Pick **Growth** or **Scale** from the plan selector and confirm your choice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the upgrade">
    Your new plan takes effect immediately. Your platform fee drops right away, and the new monthly fee applies at your next billing cycle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Already on Growth and pulling in ₮6M+/mo in member revenue? Upgrading to Scale cuts your platform fee from 5% to 2% — on ₮6,000,000 that's ₮180,000 more in your pocket every single month.
</Tip>

## Downgrade your plan

You can downgrade to a lower-tier plan at any time from your billing settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings → Billing">
    From your creator dashboard, navigate to **Settings → Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Change plan'">
    Select **Change plan** next to your current subscription.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the lower plan">
    Choose **Growth** or **Starter** and confirm your selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the downgrade">
    Your downgrade takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle. You'll continue on your current plan until then.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Downgrading increases your platform fee — you'll keep a smaller percentage of every membership payment your members make. For example, moving from Growth (5%) back to Starter (10%) means you keep 90% instead of 95% on all incoming member revenue. Make sure the lower monthly fee actually saves you money net of the higher transaction cut before you confirm.
</Warning>

## How billing works

Berrytap plans are billed monthly and renew automatically on the same day each month — for example, if you started on the 12th, you'll be billed on the 12th every month.

- **Currency:** All plan fees are charged in Mongolian tögrög (₮, MNT). No surprises from exchange rate swings.
- **Auto-renewal:** Your plan renews automatically each period. You'll receive a billing confirmation to your registered email.
- **Payment methods:** You can pay your plan fee by card (Visa/Mastercard with auto-charge) or via QPay. Manage your payment method in **Settings → Billing**.
- **Failed payments:** If your payment method fails, a grace period applies before your creator features are restricted. Update your payment method promptly to avoid interruption.
- **Payout adjustments:** Berrytap may withhold or adjust your payout balance to cover chargebacks, refunds, or suspected fraud. Your available balance and any holds are always visible in **Settings → Billing**.

## Cancel your Berrytap creator account

You can cancel your creator account at any time from your billing settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings → Billing">
    Navigate to **Settings → Billing** in your creator dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Cancel plan'">
    Scroll to the bottom of the billing page and select **Cancel plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the confirmation prompt carefully">
    Berrytap will show you exactly what happens to your community and members. Confirm when you're ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancellation takes effect">
    Your creator account is cancelled at the end of the current billing period. You won't be charged again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you cancel your plan and don't keep a plan active, **your members will lose access to your community**. Your courses, live rooms, and community feed will become inaccessible to them. If you think you might return, consider downgrading to Starter rather than cancelling entirely — it keeps your community alive at the lowest cost.
</Note>

<Note>
  Have a billing question, a charge that looks wrong, or need help with a payment error? Email [support@berrytap.com](mailto:support@berrytap.com) and the team will sort it out for you.
</Note>
