Berrytap handles the full payment loop so you can focus on building your community. Members pay in tögrög (₮) using the method that works best for them — a quick QPay scan, a saved card, or a tap with Apple Pay or Google Pay. On the other side, every successful payment flows straight into your creator earnings dashboard, minus a small platform fee. No chasing invoices, no currency headaches, no third-party checkout to stitch together yourself.

## How payments work

Every community on Berrytap is priced in **Mongolian tögrög (₮, MNT)**. Whether a member pays with a local bank app via QPay or with an international Visa card, the amount they see at checkout is always in MNT. There are no surprise currency conversions for members, and your earnings are always denominated in the same currency you set your prices in.

When a member completes a payment, Berrytap collects it on your behalf, deducts the platform fee for your plan, and adds the remainder to your creator balance. You can then request a payout to your registered bank account at any time.

## Platform fee structure

Berrytap charges a platform fee on each successful membership payment. The fee depends on which creator plan you're on — the higher the plan, the lower the fee, and the more you keep.

| Plan | Monthly plan cost | Platform fee | You keep |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | ₮15,000/mo | 10% | 90% |
| Growth | ₮40,000/mo | 5% | 95% |
| Scale | ₮500,000/mo | 2% | 98% |

<Note>
  Payment processing fees from card providers (Visa, Mastercard, etc.) may apply on top of the platform fee. These are charged by the payment processor and are separate from Berrytap's fee.
</Note>

## Payment security

Berrytap never stores your members' raw card numbers. Card credentials are handled entirely by our payment processing partners, who are certified to the highest industry security standards. Berrytap only receives a secure token that lets us manage renewals and billing — your members' sensitive data never touches our servers.
