Every time a member successfully pays for your community, Berrytap gets to work on your behalf — collecting the payment, deducting the platform fee for your plan, and adding the rest straight to your creator earnings balance. From there, you're in control: request a payout whenever you're ready and we'll transfer your earnings to your registered bank account. Here's exactly how it all works.

## How your earnings accumulate

Each successful membership payment flows through a simple calculation:

**Member pays → Berrytap deducts platform fee → Remainder added to your creator balance**

The platform fee depends on your plan:

| Plan | Monthly cost | Platform fee | You keep |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | ₮15,000/mo | 10% | 90% |
| Growth | ₮40,000/mo | 5% | 95% |
| Scale | ₮500,000/mo | 2% | 98% |

So if a member pays ₮29,000 for a monthly membership and you're on the Growth plan (5% fee), ₮27,550 lands in your creator balance. Every payment — whether it comes through QPay, a card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay — goes through the same calculation.

<Note>
  Payment processing fees from card and wallet providers may apply on top of the platform fee. These are charged by the payment processor and are separate from Berrytap's platform fee. You'll see a breakdown of any applicable fees in your earnings dashboard.
</Note>

## Viewing your earnings dashboard

Your earnings are tracked in real time in the creator dashboard. To view it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your creator dashboard">
    Log in to Berrytap and navigate to **Dashboard → Earnings**. You'll see your available balance, a full transaction history, and a breakdown of each payment received.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review individual transactions">
    Click into any transaction to see the gross payment amount, the platform fee deducted, any processing fees applied, and the net amount credited to your balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track your available payout balance">
    Your available balance — the amount you can request as a payout — is shown at the top of the earnings page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to request a payout

When you're ready to transfer your earnings to your bank account, here's the process:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your bank account details">
    Go to **Settings → Payout account** and enter your bank account details. You'll need your account number and any other details your bank requires. Double-check everything before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request a payout">
    From the **Earnings** page, click **Request payout**. Select the amount you'd like to withdraw (up to your available balance) and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manual review">
    Berrytap reviews all payout requests manually before transferring funds. This is a standard step to protect both creators and members — it ensures accuracy and guards against fraud.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transfer to your bank account">
    Once your payout is approved, the funds are transferred to the bank account you provided. You'll receive a notification when the transfer is on its way.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How long do payouts take?

Payouts are reviewed and processed manually, which means timing can vary. The actual bank transfer time after approval depends on your bank.

If your payout has been pending for longer than expected, reach out to the Berrytap support team at [support@berrytap.com](mailto:support@berrytap.com) with your payout request details.

<Warning>
  Keep your bank account details **accurate and up to date** in your payout settings. If your account number or other details are incorrect, your payout will fail or be delayed — and we can't redirect a transfer that's already been sent to a wrong account. Always double-check your details before submitting a payout request.
</Warning>

## Your tax responsibility

Berrytap pays you your earnings — but it's on you to handle what comes next. As a creator, you are solely responsible for reporting your earnings to the relevant tax authorities and paying any taxes owed on your income. Berrytap does not withhold tax on your behalf or file tax returns for you.

<Tip>
  Keep records of your earnings transactions from your dashboard. If you're unsure about your tax obligations as a content creator or community operator, consult a local tax professional.
</Tip>

## Withheld or adjusted payouts

In certain situations, Berrytap may withhold or adjust your payout. This can happen when:

- A member successfully disputes a charge (**chargeback**) with their bank — the disputed amount is held until the chargeback is resolved.
- A **refund** has been issued to a member, either because you approved it or because it was required by law.
- There is **suspected fraud** or unusual activity on your account that requires investigation.

Berrytap will notify you if your payout is affected and work with you to resolve the situation as quickly as possible.
