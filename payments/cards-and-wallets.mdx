Card payments on Berrytap are built for convenience — members save their card once and auto-renewal takes care of the rest. No logging back in every month, no manual invoice to chase. And for members using Apple Pay or Google Pay, checkout is even faster: one tap and they're in. Everything is charged in MNT, so what members see at checkout is exactly what they pay — no conversion guesswork.

## Supported cards and wallets

Berrytap supports the following payment methods at card checkout:

- **Visa** — credit and debit cards
- **Mastercard** — credit and debit cards
- **Apple Pay** — for members on iPhone, iPad, or Mac with Apple Pay set up
- **Google Pay** — for members on Android or Chrome with Google Pay set up

All methods are charged in **Mongolian tögrög (₮, MNT)**. Your bank may handle any underlying currency conversion if your card is issued in another currency, but the amount Berrytap charges is always in MNT.

## Auto-renewal for memberships

When a member pays for a recurring membership by card, they can save their card details and enable auto-renewal. Here's what that looks like:

<Steps>
  <Step title="First checkout">
    The member enters their card details (or selects Apple Pay / Google Pay) at checkout and agrees to auto-renewal. Their card is saved securely by Berrytap's payment processor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automatic charge at each period">
    At the start of every billing period, Berrytap automatically charges the saved card for the membership amount in MNT. The member doesn't need to do anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access continues">
    As long as the charge succeeds, the member's access renews seamlessly and they stay in the community without interruption.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## One-tap checkout with Apple Pay & Google Pay

Members who have Apple Pay or Google Pay set up on their device can breeze through checkout without typing anything. When they reach the payment step, they'll see their wallet as an option alongside standard card entry. A single biometric confirmation (Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint) is all it takes.

<Tip>
  Members using Apple Pay or Google Pay still benefit from auto-renewal just like card members — their wallet handles the recurring charge automatically each period.
</Tip>

## What happens if a card payment fails

Expired cards, insufficient funds, or bank-side declines can all cause a charge to fail. Berrytap handles this gracefully:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Payment attempt fails">
    The automatic charge at the start of the billing period is declined by the card provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grace period begins">
    A grace period kicks in — the member retains access while they have a chance to sort out their payment method.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Member updates their card">
    The member should update their saved card details in account settings before the grace period ends (see below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access may be suspended">
    If payment isn't successfully collected by the end of the grace period, access to the community may be suspended until the outstanding payment is made.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to update a saved card

If a member's card has expired or they want to switch to a different card, they can update it at any time:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to account settings">
    The member opens their Berrytap account and navigates to **Settings → Payment methods**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update or replace the card">
    They can edit the existing card details or add a new card to replace it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Once saved, the new card will be used for all future auto-renewal charges.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to turn off auto-renewal

Members can turn off auto-renewal for any membership at any time from their settings:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to account settings">
    Navigate to **Settings → Memberships** and find the community whose renewal you want to stop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disable auto-renewal">
    Toggle off auto-renewal for that membership.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access runs until the end of the period">
    The current billing period continues as normal — access doesn't cut off immediately. At the end of the period, the membership simply won't renew.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Turning off auto-renewal does **not** refund the current billing period. You'll keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for — after that, the membership ends.
</Warning>

## Creator perspective: nothing to do

If you're a creator, card and wallet payments are fully hands-off. When a member checks out with a card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay, Berrytap processes the payment automatically — at first checkout and at every subsequent renewal. You don't need to send invoices, follow up on renewals, or take any action. Your earnings from each successful charge appear in your creator dashboard automatically.

<Note>
  Payment processing fees charged by card providers (Visa, Mastercard, and wallet providers) may apply in addition to Berrytap's platform fee. These fees are set by the payment processor, not by Berrytap, and may vary by card type or issuing bank.
</Note>
