QPay is the go-to payment method for people in Mongolia — it's built right into the major bank apps, familiar to almost every local user, and takes just a few seconds to complete. Berrytap has QPay built in from day one, so any Mongolian member who visits your community checkout can pay instantly with the app they already have on their phone. No extra steps, no foreign card required.

## What is QPay?

QPay is Mongolia's national QR-based bank payment network. Instead of entering card details, a member opens their bank app, scans a QR code generated at checkout, and confirms the payment amount — all within their existing banking app. The funds settle directly from their bank account to Berrytap, and access to your community is granted right away.

## QPay for one-time access

When a member purchases a one-time course or single-access product, the checkout flow is straightforward:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Member reaches checkout">
    The member selects QPay as their payment method on the checkout page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="QR code is generated">
    Berrytap generates a unique QPay QR code for that transaction, displayed on screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Member scans and confirms">
    The member opens their bank app, scans the QR code, and confirms the payment amount in MNT.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access is granted">
    Once the payment clears, the member's access is activated immediately and they're taken into the community, course, or content they purchased.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## QPay for recurring memberships

Recurring memberships work a little differently with QPay compared to card auto-renewal. Because QPay is a manual, scan-to-pay method, it can't be charged automatically in the background.

Here's how each renewal works:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invoice is issued">
    At the start of each new billing period, Berrytap automatically issues a new QPay invoice to the member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Member pays manually">
    The member receives the invoice, opens it, and scans the QPay QR code in their bank app to complete payment — just like the first time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access continues">
    Once payment is confirmed, the membership renews and the member's access continues uninterrupted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grace period applies">
    If the member doesn't pay by the due date, a grace period kicks in. If payment isn't made within the grace period, access may be suspended until they pay.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  QPay recurring payments are **not automatic** — members must actively pay each invoice at renewal. This is different from card auto-renewal, where the charge happens in the background. Make sure your members know they'll need to pay each period to keep their access active.
</Note>

## Setting up QPay as a creator

Good news: you don't need to do anything. QPay is built into Berrytap's checkout and is available to all communities automatically. There's no integration to configure, no API keys to enter, and no extra approval process. When a member in Mongolia reaches your checkout, QPay will already be there as an option.

## What the member checkout experience looks like

From a member's point of view, paying with QPay on Berrytap is quick and familiar:

1. They browse to your community page and click **Join** or **Buy**.
2. At checkout, they select **QPay** from the payment options.
3. A QR code appears on screen — sized for easy scanning.
4. They open their bank app (Khan Bank, Golomt, Trade and Development Bank, or any QPay-supported app), tap the scan option, and point their camera at the QR code.
5. Their app shows the payment amount in MNT. They confirm with a tap.
6. Payment is complete — they're in.

The whole flow typically takes under 30 seconds for a member who already has their bank app open.

<Tip>
  For recurring memberships, give your members a heads-up when renewal invoices are due. Post a reminder in your community feed a day or two before the billing date — something like "Renewal invoices are out! Scan your QPay QR to keep your access active 🙌" keeps members engaged and reduces accidental lapses.
</Tip>
